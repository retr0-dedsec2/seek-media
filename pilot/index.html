Seek Free pilot for solo founders

If people are asking for what you built, I’ll find where.

You built a product and customers are slow to come. I look for recent public conversations where someone describes the problem you solve, and I send you the ones that hold up. It’s free while I test the format.

What you get

Within 7 days of your answers to three short questions:

  • Up to 10 public conversations from the last 30 days where someone asks for what your product does.
  • For each one: the link, the date, the key quote, why it fits, what it doesn’t prove, and a suggested first line.
  • Only those that hold up. I check each author and drop promotional accounts. If I find three, I tell you three.

What it is not

  • Not a promise of customers. It’s research. You do the outreach, in your own name.
  • Nothing is posted or sent for you.
  • No card, no call. Everything happens by email.

How it’s done

I use AI tools to search. Every conversation is read, and its author checked, before it reaches you.

What I ask in return

A week after delivery, tell me how many people you contacted and how many replied. If it helped, one honest sentence I can quote.

Why it’s free

I’m testing this format and I have no results to show yet. Yours would be among the first. I take a few founders at a time: if I’m full, I’ll tell you when.
